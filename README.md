### About me

- 🔐 I design digital chips and take them to measured silicon. My main project, **AEGIS**, is an all-digital random number generator in TSMC 40nm, measured at **691.2 Gb/s** and **40.2 fJ/bit**.
- 🔬 I want to build AI accelerators with security designed into the architecture, such as side-channel and fault-attack resistance.
- 🏎️ I led the electrical division of NTHU Racing, a ~70-member Formula Student EV team ranked No. 1 in Asia.
- 🎓 I am applying to EECS Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.
